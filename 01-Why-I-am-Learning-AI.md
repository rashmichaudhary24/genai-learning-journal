# Why I am Learning AI

## Background

I have spent over two decades working in learning and development, training, digital transformation, and organizational capability building. As artificial intelligence continues to transform industries and workplaces, I believe that understanding AI is no longer optional for leaders.

## Why I Started This Journey

My goal is not to become a software engineer or machine learning researcher. Instead, I want to understand:

- How AI systems work
- How organizations adopt AI
- The opportunities and risks associated with AI
- AI governance and responsible AI practices
- The role of AI in enterprise transformation

## Areas I Am Exploring

- Generative AI
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI strategy
- AI adoption
- AI governance
- AI agents
- Enterprise AI applications

## My Approach

This repository serves as my public learning journal. I intend to document concepts, summarize resources, record observations, and reflect on how AI may transform organizations and learning ecosystems.

## Current Status

I am currently studying foundational concepts related to:
- Open Source AI
- Large Language Models
- Tokens and Context Windows
- Prompt Engineering
- AI Trends and Governance
